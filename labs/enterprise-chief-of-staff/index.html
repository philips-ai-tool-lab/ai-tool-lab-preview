Chief of Staff

创建一名连接 Outlook、Teams 和 SharePoint 的个人 Chief of Staff，并将每周优先事项转化为可执行简报。

约 35 分钟入门Outlook · Teams · SharePoint

练习目标

一名个人 Chief of Staff、一次来源连接检查，以及一份每周自动生成的管理层简报。

推荐方式：ChatGPT Workspace Agent Builder + 已连接的 Microsoft Apps

练习结果— 一名已创建的 Workspace Agent— 已核对的 Microsoft 连接— 一份每周周期简报与首次任务输出
BUILD

创建个人 Chief of Staff

先建立 Agent，再检查其连接和输出边界。

01

从 Builder 开始

在 ChatGPT 中选择 Build a new agent；不要在空白对话中直接开始。粘贴构建 Prompt，让 Builder 先生成计划。

CHIEF OF STAFF BUILD PROMPT
Help me build a personal Chief of Staff Workspace Agent for my role at Philips.

I want this agent to help me:
- prepare for important leadership discussions,
- turn business priorities, one-pagers, meeting notes, and relevant internal documents into concise executive briefs,
- surface risks, trade-offs, and decisions that need my attention,
- draft clear updates for my team and stakeholders,
- help me stay on top of the most important open loops each week.

Use the connected Philips sources that are available to me, especially Outlook Email, Outlook Calendar, Teams, and SharePoint / OneDrive. Prioritize internal sources over the open web unless I explicitly ask for external context.

Keep human judgment in the loop for decisions, sensitive topics, external communications, and anything involving confidential or regulated information.

Build the first draft of the agent for me, show me the proposed plan before creating it, and tell me which account connections I still need to enable before I press Create.
02

回答简短设置问题

依次确认默认输出位置、工作重点与需要的连接。信息不确定时保留人工判断点。

03

核对草稿与 Microsoft 连接

检查草稿中是否包含 Outlook Calendar、Outlook Email、Teams 和 SharePoint / OneDrive；缺少连接时先补齐再创建。

04

创建并打开 Agent

确认计划和连接无误后创建 Agent，并用 Try in ChatGPT 打开首次任务。

05

安排每周 Chief of Staff brief

复制每周简报 Prompt，并确认使用当前 ChatGPT 用户配置的时区。Agent 可能会先要求批准创建日程。

WEEKLY BRIEF PROMPT
Every Monday at 07:30 in my current timezone, prepare my weekly Chief of Staff brief.

Use my Outlook Calendar, Outlook Email, Teams, and SharePoint / OneDrive to summarize:
1. the three most important leadership priorities for the week,
2. the meetings that need my preparation,
3. the open loops or decisions that require my attention,
4. one thing I should delegate, unblock, or escalate.

Keep it to one page. Make the brief practical, not generic, and include the source trail I should inspect if I want to go deeper.
06

运行一个真实任务

上传一份已允许使用的优先事项材料，或选择一场领导评审；检查简报是否区分事实、假设、风险和下一步。

SCHEDULE

让重要事项按周浮现

用一个可复核的周期简报减少遗漏的开放事项。

创建 Chief of Staff

Help me build a personal Chief of Staff Workspace Agent for my role at Philips. Use connected Outlook Email, Outlook Calendar, Teams, and SharePoint / OneDrive to prepare leadership briefs, surface open decisions and draft stakeholder updates. Show the proposed plan and missing connections before I press Create. Keep human judgment in the loop for sensitive decisions and external communication.

生成每周简报

Every Monday at 07:30 in my current timezone, prepare my weekly Chief of Staff brief. Use Outlook Calendar, Outlook Email, Teams, and SharePoint / OneDrive to summarize: 1) three leadership priorities, 2) meetings needing preparation, 3) open loops or decisions, and 4) one item to delegate, unblock, or escalate. Keep it to one practical page with a source trail.

准备领导评审

Help me prepare for one upcoming leadership review. First ask which meeting and priority matter most. Then use my calendar and relevant internal context to give five questions to ask, likely tensions, risks to surface early, and the decision to drive by the end of the meeting.
