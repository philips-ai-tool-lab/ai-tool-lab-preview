# 绿色办公周练习资料说明 / Green Office Week Exercise Materials

## 中文说明

三份文件用于“绿色办公周”项目接手练习，均为虚构培训资料：

- **项目简介**：介绍项目背景、目标、成功指标、预算、活动范围和关键日期。
- **会议纪要**：记录已确认的决定、各角色的行动项，以及仍需讨论和确认的问题。
- **时间表**：列出主要里程碑、截止日期和记录的进度状态。

请将三份TXT文件一起上传至Copilot，比较不同来源的信息，并整理出项目接手简报和跟进邮件草稿。资料中有意保留日期冲突和信息缺口，用于练习识别问题、注明来源及保留待确认事项。请勿自行补充缺失事实，也不要将这些资料用于实际业务。

## English Description

The three files support the Green Office Week project handover exercise. All content is fictional and provided for training purposes:

- **Project Brief:** Introduces the project background, objectives, success measures, budget, scope, and key dates.
- **Meeting Notes:** Records confirmed decisions, action items assigned to different roles, and questions that remain open.
- **Timeline:** Lists the main milestones, deadlines, and recorded progress statuses.

Please upload all three TXT files to Copilot, compare the information across sources, and prepare a project handover brief and a draft follow-up email. The materials intentionally contain conflicting dates and information gaps so you can practise identifying issues, citing sources, and flagging items for confirmation. Do not invent missing facts or use these materials for actual business activities.
